SUITE STANDARDS

The Sovereign Suite Guarantee

Every tool launched under the Treen banner shares three non-negotiable engineering principles.

Zero Storage Permissions

Applications operate solely within their scoped app-private sandbox. They do not request broad media or file storage access.

No Telemetry Network Calls

Binaries are shipped without HTTP clients, tracking beacons, or ad-network dependencies. Offline means offline.

Authenticated Backups

State can be archived into cryptographic .popvault files. Encrypted and signed to guarantee data permanence.
