SYSTEM RULES

Core Mechanics

Clean operational rules that govern daily tracking, habit continuity, and milestone progression.

MECHANIC 01

Daily Planning

Simple, focused task lists with sub-checkpoints and completion timestamps. Each item records the exact moment of completion in local SQLite storage, eliminating retrospective second-guessing.

MECHANIC 02

Momentum

Completing 3 or more tasks in a calendar day qualifies for that day's milestone badge and maintains streak consistency. Your streak grows with daily consistency.

MECHANIC 03

Midnight Evaluation

Handled quietly after 11:59 PM with no disruptive real-time pressure or notifications. Streaks and rewards are calculated automatically in the local database while you sleep.

MECHANIC 04

6 Themed Universes

Batman/DC, Marvel, Shonen, Manhwa, Cyberpunk, and Anti-Heroes. Theme switching remains locked until all 6 milestone tiers in the active pack are completed.
