FUNCTIONAL SPECIFICATION

Product Highlights

Every feature is calibrated to assist focus rather than create digital friction.

SUBSYSTEM 01

Daily Planning

Simple, focused list with sub-checkpoints and completion timestamps. Prevents backlogs from accumulating into endless, demoralizing queues.

SUBSYSTEM 02

Streak & Habit Logic

Completing 3 or more daily tasks maintains streak momentum and logs a milestone badge at midnight. Days with fewer than 3 completed tasks reset the counter.

SUBSYSTEM 03

Themed Collections

Six curated milestone sets spanning distinct narrative traditions. Users remain locked to their active collection until completing all progression tiers.

CURATED MILESTONE SETS
